Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 5umm0n3r-5c4nn3r (the “Service”), available at https://5umm0n3r5-5c4nn3r.dev/, handles information when you look up a League of Legends summoner. The Service is an independent, non-commercial fan project operated by an individual based in France.

Short version: we don't use cookies, we don't run analytics or tracking, and we don't store your searches on our servers. When you look up a summoner, your request is forwarded to Riot Games in real time and the result is shown to you — nothing is kept on our side. The only thing saved is the last Riot ID and region you looked up, stored locally in your own browser so you stay signed in — it never leaves your device, and signing out erases it.

1. Who is responsible (Data Controller)

The Service is operated by an individual (“we”, “us”, the “operator”). For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact:

  • Email: sirtristanus@gmail.com

2. What information we process

To provide the lookup you request, the Service processes:

  • The Riot ID and region you enter — the game name, tag line, and platform region you type into the search field. This may be your own account or any account you choose to look up.
  • The data returned by Riot Games — account information (PUUID, game name, tag line), match history, and ranked league data associated with the Riot ID you searched. This is public game data.
  • Technical connection data — like any website, our hosting provider automatically receives your IP address and basic request metadata (browser type, timestamp) when your browser connects.
  • Your last lookup, stored in your browser — the Riot ID and region from your most recent successful lookup are saved in your browser's local storage so that the Service can keep you signed in across page reloads. This stays on your device, is never sent to or readable by us, and is removed when you sign out.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not store your searches, the Riot IDs you enter, or any results on our servers, in a database, or in a cache.
  • We do not set cookies. We use your browser's local storage only to remember your last lookup so you stay signed in (see section 2); we cannot read it.
  • We do not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • We do not require you to create an account or provide an email to use the Service.
  • We do not sell or rent any data to anyone.

4. How your data is used and shared

The Riot ID and region you enter are sent to our backend, which forwards them to the Riot Games API to retrieve the requested account, match, and ranked data. That data is returned to your browser and displayed. It is not retained afterwards.

We rely on the following third parties to operate the Service:

  • Riot Games, Inc. — provides the underlying game data. Your search is processed under Riot's Privacy Notice.
  • Vercel Inc. — hosts the website and backend. Vercel processes connection data (e.g. IP address, request logs) on our behalf to deliver and secure the Service. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

As we are based in the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies. We process the limited data described above on the basis of our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in providing the lookup you actively requested and in keeping the Service secure and operational. The processing is minimal, transient, and limited to what is necessary to answer your request.

6. International transfers

Riot Games operates globally and may process the requested data outside the EU (including in the United States). By using the Service to query Riot's API, the data necessary for your lookup is transferred to Riot under their own safeguards and privacy terms.

7. Data retention

We do not retain the data you submit on our side. It exists only for the moment needed to fulfil your request. Transient server logs that may contain your IP address are handled by our hosting provider (Vercel) according to their retention practices, and are used only for operating and securing the Service.

The single exception is the last Riot ID and region you looked up, which is kept in your own browser's local storage to keep you signed in. It remains there until you sign out, clear your browser's site data, or look up a different summoner. We have no access to it and keep no copy.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because we do not store the data you submit, there is generally no record on our side to access, correct, or delete — but you may still contact us with any request at sirtristanus@gmail.com. You can erase the lookup saved in your browser at any time by signing out or clearing your browser's site data.

Note that match history, ranked standing, and Riot IDs are public game data controlled by Riot Games. To exercise rights over the game data itself, you should contact Riot Games directly.

If you believe your data has been handled improperly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 15 and we do not knowingly process their personal data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date above. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the revised policy.
